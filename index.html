FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question that's not answered here? Reach out — we're happy to help.

What subjects do you tutor?+

We cover all K-12 subjects, with a focus on math, science, English/writing, SAT/ACT prep, and college essay coaching. See our Subjects & Rates page for the full list.

What grade levels do you work with?+

We tutor students from elementary school through 12th grade, plus college essay coaching for graduating seniors.

Do you offer online tutoring, in-person, or both?+

Both! Many of our tutors offer online sessions, and some also tutor in person depending on location. Check each tutor's profile on our Our Tutors page for details.

How do I book a session?+

Fill out the form on our Contact page with your student's grade, subject, and availability, and we'll follow up to schedule a first session.

What are your rates?+

Rates vary by subject and session length. See our Subjects & Rates page for current pricing, including package discounts.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?+

We ask for at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule a session whenever possible. Contact your tutor directly or reach out to us to make changes.
