What it's like to tutor with us

As a Top Hat Tutor, you'll work one-on-one with younger students in your strongest subjects, building a schedule that fits around your own classes, sports, and activities. Most tutors meet with a student for an hour or two a week, either online or in person, and get ongoing support from our team along the way.

It's flexible, it pays well, and it looks great on a college application, but most tutors say the best part is watching a student go from stuck to confident.

  • Make $50 an hour
  • A flexible schedule that fits around school
  • Real teaching & mentorship experience
  • Support and training from our team

Who we're looking for

  • Upperclassmen (grades 11 and 12)
  • Living in or near Fairfield County (Westport, Fairfield, Norwalk, Darien, Weston, Wilton & more)
  • Strong grades in the subjects you'd like to tutor
  • Patient, reliable, and good with younger students
