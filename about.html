Our Story

How Top Hat Tutors Got Started

Top Hat Tutors was founded in 2013 in Westport, Connecticut, on a simple idea: the best tutor for a student is often someone who just took the class themselves and still remembers exactly where it gets hard. What started as a handful of local high schoolers helping younger students in their own neighborhood has grown into a trusted tutoring network serving families across Fairfield County, including Westport, Fairfield, Norwalk, Darien, Weston, and Wilton.

[Placeholder] Over the years, we've stayed true to that original idea. Every Top Hat Tutor is a reliable, high-achieving upperclassman who has actually taken the class they're teaching, so they know exactly what a student needs to succeed, not just the material, but the specific teacher, workload, and pace of the class itself.
