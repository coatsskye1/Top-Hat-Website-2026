Our Story

Our Story

Top Hat Tutors was founded in 2013 in Westport, Connecticut by Charlie Jersey. What started as a handful of local high schoolers helping younger students in their own neighborhood has grown into a trusted tutoring network serving families across Fairfield County, including Westport, Fairfield, Norwalk, Darien, Weston, and Wilton.

Our tutors know what it's like to struggle through classes in the high pressure environment of Fairfield County. Whether it's a single session or weekly support you need, we are happy to help you find a tutor!
